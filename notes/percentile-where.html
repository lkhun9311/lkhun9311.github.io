Which one to read

Performance measurement · #Percentile

← Percentile overview

p50, p95 and p99 answer different questions. Read one and some questions have no answer; read all three and the problem narrows.

What each one answers#

  • p50 · how long it usually takes. Bad here means everyone is slow.
  • p95 · the bad case people meet often. One time in twenty.
  • p99 · the tail. Unusually long here means the cause is somewhere else entirely.

What the distance between p50 and p99 says#

Close together means the whole thing is evenly slow. The code simply takes that long.

Far apart means most requests are fast and some are blocked. The cause is then usually not the average code path but queueing: a lock, a shortage of connections, retries, GC.

The volume list article on this site had that shape. The median got 21 times faster while p99 got 1.9 times worse. The two numbers moved in opposite directions, and that gap is what sent the search toward a lock.

Why one number is not enough#

Report only p99 and there is no way to tell whether everything got worse or only the tail did. Report only p50 and the tail is invisible.

And say whether failed requests were included. Measure with timed-out requests excluded and p99 looks better, but what happened is that the slow requests were deleted.
