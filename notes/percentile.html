Percentile

Performance measurement · #Percentile

Contents

  • What it is
  • How it is produced
  • Why not the average
  • Where to go next

A percentile is the value at a given position once the values are sorted. p99 is the value at the position 99% of the way along: 99 of every 100 requests were faster.

What it is#

The first number anyone reaches for on response time is the average: everything added up and divided by the count. Nobody may have actually experienced that value.

A percentile is different. The values are sorted smallest first and one position is pointed at. p50 is the middle, p95 is 95% along, p99 is 99% along. Because it is chosen rather than computed, a request that produced it exists.

How it is produced#

Four steps: collect the time each request took, sort them, pick the position, read the value.

A choice hides in the third step. With 20 samples the 99% position is the 19.8th, and there is no 19.8th value. How that position is settled differs between tools. That is in There are two ways to compute it.

Why not the average#

An average buries the slow ones. With 99 requests at 10ms and one at 10s the average is about 110ms. Most of the screen is fast, and the average makes all of it look slightly slow; meanwhile the one slow request disappears inside it.

And that one is not rare. At a million requests a day, beyond p99 is ten thousand requests. The same person meets it several times a day.
